From the founder's desk

Built by a researcher,
for researchers.

Shailendra Dhakad

Founder & CEO, CoAuthorLab · Researcher, Autonomous Systems & Robotics

Shailendra thinks about problems the way an engineer sketches before building — starting from first principles, testing ideas quickly, and refining until something actually holds up in the real world. That instinct for turning a raw idea into a working system is what shapes his research in autonomous systems and robotics, and it's the same instinct behind CoAuthorLab: a space built for people who have an idea and need the structure to carry it further.

  • “Research is a journey, not a checklist.”
  • “An idea is only as good as the structure that carries it forward.”
